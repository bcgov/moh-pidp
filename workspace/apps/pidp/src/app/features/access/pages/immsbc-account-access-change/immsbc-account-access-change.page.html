ImmsBC - Account Access Change

Welcome to the ImmsBC account access level change request form.

Please note that this form can only be filled out by the pharmacy manager. If you are not the pharmacy manager, please ask the pharmacy manager to fill out this form for you.

Please use this form if your staff member already has an ImmsBC account and require an access level change. Please do not use this form to request accounts.

Requests may take 2-4 business days to process. After changes are made, it will take up to 40 minutes for the changes to be pushed onto ImmsBC.
