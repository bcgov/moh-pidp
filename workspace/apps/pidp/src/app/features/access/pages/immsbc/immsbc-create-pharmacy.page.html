ImmsBC - Registration Form

All pharmacies providing publicly funded influenza vaccines are required to use ImmsBC to manage all aspects of the administration of flu and COVID-19 vaccines to British Columbians.

ImmsBC is used for scheduling, managing appointments, entering vaccine records and inventory. Patients are able to see if your pharmacy provides influenza vaccines, COVID-19 vaccines or both vaccination types prior to booking.

For pharmacies that currently have access to ImmsBC, please manage your existing pharmacy here

For pharmacies not currently using ImmsBC, please register your pharmacy for ImmsBC access by filling in this form.

Before you fill out this form, please ensure the following pre-requisites have been met:

  • CareConnect Privacy and Security Agreement is completed and a screenshot is available to be uploaded with your registration form.
    • In order to gain access to the ImmsBC system, your pharmacy will need to have submitted the CareConnect Privacy and Security Agreement for your pharmacy. Please login to my.careconnect.ca and collect a screenshot of your CareConnect Privacy and Security Agreement Although ImmsBC and CareConnect are independent systems both systems are hosted by PHSA. By completing the CareConnect Privacy and Security Agreement, you are meeting the privacy and security requirements for your pharmacy in order to gain access to ImmsBC also.
  • Pharmacy is registered in good standing with the College of Pharmacists of BC as a community pharmacy.
  • You are the registered pharmacy manager at the pharmacy.
  • Your pharmacy is enrolled with Pharmacare with a PharmaCare Code.
