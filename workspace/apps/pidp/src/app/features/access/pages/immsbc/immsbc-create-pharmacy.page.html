ImmsBC - Add Pharmacy

Welcome to the ImmsBC account creation form.

Please use this form if your staff member does not have access to ImmsBC. After your request has been submitted, if the pharmacy has been on-boarded, the ImmsBC account will be created and the credentials as well as additional information will be released directly to the staff member. It will take 2-3 business days for processing.

Before you fill out this form, please ensure the following pre-requisites have been met:

  • Staff member has a smartphone capable of Microsoft Authenticator
  • Staff member College ID (if pharmacist)
  • Staff member has a valid BCPhA account
  • Staff member has created a @bcprovider.ca account through OneHealth ID
  • ImmsBC training completed on the BCPhA eTraining Portal

IMPORTANT: Please note that the user should have completed the ImmsBC course on the eTraining portal. The eTraining portal is available at https://www.bcpharmacy.ca/etraining/. If the staff member does not have a BCPhA account, please create an account here.
